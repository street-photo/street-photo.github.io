Philosophy

Street photography is not merely documentation—it is interpretation. It asks us to find meaning in chaos, beauty in the mundane, and humanity in fleeting moments.

The best street photographs reveal truths that exist just beneath the surface of everyday life. They make the familiar strange and the strange familiar.

Learn from the Masters

Henri Cartier-Bresson

The father of modern photojournalism, who taught us about the "decisive moment"—that fraction of a second when all elements align.

Vivian Maier

A nanny who secretly created one of the most remarkable bodies of street photography, discovered only after her death.

Garry Winogrand

Prolific chronicler of American life whose tilted frames and raw energy redefined street photography aesthetics.

Daido Moriyama

Japanese master of high-contrast, grainy imagery that captures the frenetic energy of urban existence.
