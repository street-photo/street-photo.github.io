The Tutorial Library

A curated collection of 33 street photography lessons, from first principles to ethics, editing, history, projects, and personal style.

How to Use This Collection

If you are new to street photography, start with the foundations: composition, light, the decisive moment, storytelling, and technique. After that, branch into color, ethics, observation, and projects according to the kind of work you want to make.

Suggested Paths

Beginner path: Composition → Light → The Decisive Moment → Storytelling → Technical Mastery.

People path: Ethics → Observation → Psychology → Street Portraits → Documentary.

Authorial path: Editing → Critique → Personal Style → Projects & Series → History.

Advanced path: Reflections → Crowds → Humor → Visual Metaphor → Flash & Artificial Light.
